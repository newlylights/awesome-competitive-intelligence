# Competitive Battle Card Template 🛡️

> A practical, sales-ready template for building competitive battle cards that win deals.

---

## What is a Battle Card?

A **battle card** is a concise, one-page competitive reference that equips sales teams with the intelligence they need to handle competitive situations during sales conversations. According to [Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/sales/insights/sales-enablement), organizations with well-maintained battle cards see up to 20% higher win rates in competitive deals.

## 5. DISCOVERY QUESTIONS

Use these to surface competitor weaknesses during discovery:

1. "How are you currently handling [pain point competitor doesn't solve]?"
2. "What's your biggest frustration with your current [category] solution?"
3. "How important is [our key differentiator] to your team's workflow?"
4. "What would happen if [gap risk scenario]?"
