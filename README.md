# Awesome Competitive Intelligence 🕵️

> A curated list of competitive intelligence resources, frameworks, templates, and tools — for product managers, strategists, marketers, and CI analysts.

**Competitive intelligence (CI)** is the systematic collection, analysis, and application of information about competitors, market trends, and industry dynamics to inform strategic decision-making. Organizations with mature CI programs are [3x more likely](https://www.gartner.com/en/marketing/insights/daily-insights/why-cmos-must-lead-competitive-intelligence) to outperform revenue targets (Gartner).

## Books & Reading

- *Competitive Strategy* — Michael E. Porter (1980) — The foundational text on competitive analysis
- *Playing to Win* — A.G. Lafley & Roger L. Martin — Strategy framework for competitive advantage
- *The Art of War* — Sun Tzu — Timeless principles applied to modern business competition
- *Blue Ocean Strategy* — W. Chan Kim & Renée Mauborgne — Creating uncontested market space
- *Competitive Intelligence Advantage* — Seena Sharp — Practical CI for business decisions
