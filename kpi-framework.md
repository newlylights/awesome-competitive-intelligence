# Competitive Intelligence KPI Framework 📐

> How to measure, prove, and optimize the ROI of your competitive intelligence program.

---

## The CI Measurement Challenge

[Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/marketing/insights/articles/why-your-competitive-intelligence-program-isnt-working) reports that only 29% of CI programs can quantitatively demonstrate ROI — yet the same research shows that organizations measuring CI impact are 4x more likely to secure increased CI investment year over year.

## KPI Framework: The CI Impact Pyramid

```
           ┌──────────────────┐
           │  REVENUE IMPACT   │ ← Ultimate goal
           │  ───────────────  │
           │  • Win rate lift  │
           │  • Deal influence │
           │  • Retention     │
           ├──────────────────┤
           │  ACTIVATION       │ ← Are people USING intel?
           │  ───────────────  │
           │  • Intel adoption │
           │  • Battle card use│
           │  • Alert response │
           ├──────────────────┤
           │  OUTPUT           │ ← What are you PRODUCING?
           │  ───────────────  │
           │  • Intel volume   │
           │  • Coverage       │
           │  • Speed-to-alert │
           └──────────────────┘
```

## Tier 1: Output Metrics (Operational Efficiency)

Measure what the CI function produces.

| KPI | Formula | Target | Measurement |
|-----|---------|--------|-------------|
| **Intelligence Volume** | # of intelligence items published / month | >50 | CI platform analytics |
| **Competitor Coverage** | # of competitors with active monitoring / total competitors | 100% | Platform config |
| **Signal Sources** | # of data sources actively monitored | >10 | Source inventory |
| **Speed-to-Alert** | Time from competitor event → intel published | <4 hours | Timestamp delta |
| **Intel Accuracy Rate** | % of intel items verified / flagged as inaccurate | >95% | QA sampling |
| **Publishing Cadence** | % of scheduled deliverables shipped on time | >90% | Deliverable tracker |

---

## Tier 2: Activation Metrics (Adoption & Engagement)

Measure whether the intelligence is being used.

| KPI | Formula | Target | Measurement |
|-----|---------|--------|-------------|
| **Intel Adoption Rate** | # of unique intelligence consumers / total target audience | >60% | Platform analytics, email opens |
| **Battle Card Utilization** | # of battle card views / # of competitive deals | >80% | CRM integration, platform analytics |
| **Alert Response Rate** | % of alerts acknowledged/acted on within SLA | >70% within 24h | Alert tracking system |
| **Intel NPS** | Internal NPS survey: "How valuable is CI to your role?" | >30 | Quarterly survey |
| **Newsletter Engagement** | Open rate / click rate / forward rate | 40% / 15% / 5% | Email analytics |
| **Stakeholder Meetings** | # of strategic decisions citing CI input | >3/quarter | Decision log, meeting notes |

---

## Tier 3: Revenue Impact Metrics (Business Outcomes)

Measure how CI contributes to bottom-line results.

| KPI | Formula | Target | Measurement |
|-----|---------|--------|-------------|
| **Win Rate Lift** | (Win rate vs competitor in periods WITH intel) - (Win rate WITHOUT) | >+5% | CRM + CI platform correlation |
| **Deal Influence** | % of won deals where CI was cited as a factor | >30% | CRM field, sales survey |
| **Revenue Protected** | $ value of competitive deals where intel prevented loss | [Track] | CRM pipeline analysis |
| **Time-to-Competitive-Response** | Days from competitor move → our strategic response | <7 days | Event tracking |
| **Churn Prevention** | # of at-risk accounts saved through competitive intelligence | [Track] | CSM feedback loop |
| **Pricing Optimization** | Revenue impact from CI-informed pricing changes | [$ Track] | Pricing committee decisions |

## CI ROI Calculation Model

### Direct ROI

```
CI ROI = (Revenue Impact from CI − CI Program Cost) / CI Program Cost × 100%

Where:
• Revenue Impact = (Deal Influence Revenue × 0.15 attribution)
                   + Revenue Protected from Churn Prevention
                   + Pricing Optimization Uplift

• CI Program Cost = Platform subscriptions ($[X]/year)
                  + CI team allocation ($[X]/year)
                  + Data source subscriptions ($[X]/year)
```

### Example Calculation

| Component | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| CI Platform (FollowEngine) | $12,000/yr |
| CI Analyst (0.5 FTE) | $45,000/yr |
| Data Sources | $8,000/yr |
| **Total Cost** | **$65,000/yr** |
| | |
| Deal Influence ($5M × 0.15) | $750,000 |
| Churn Prevention | $200,000 |
| Pricing Optimization | $150,000 |
| **Total Revenue Impact** | **$1,100,000** |
| | |
| **CI ROI** | **1,592%** |
